## Working

- A few weeks back, we opened waitlist for [Feedhammer](https://feedhammer.com)! If you use Basecamp or know someone who does, please suggest them to check out Feedhammer. They may find it helpful. In essence, Feedhammer aggregates a collection of Basecamp projects, cleans them up, filters them as per your choice and gives you one feed URL that you can add to Google Calendar or share with a client. They don't have to keep checking Basecamp for any upcoming Meetings, Todos or Cards. Feedhammer is our answer to a very specific problem that most Basecamp users, especially Freelancers and Agency owners are feeling. We will be launching in a few weeks time.
